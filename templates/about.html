Problem Statement & Objectives

Traditional online examinations suffer from vulnerabilities such as impersonation, unauthorized peer assistance, tab switching, and out-of-camera references. Manual remote proctoring via 1-on-1 human invigilation is expensive and unscalable.

Primary Objectives:

  • Automate continuous invigilation using localized, CPU-efficient Computer Vision algorithms.
  • Detect facial absence, multiple persons, diverted head pose, and gaze deviation.
  • Eliminate browser cheating via fullscreen locks, tab-switching interceptors, and clipboard disabling.
  • Calculate dynamic, calibrated risk scores while maintaining audit logs and photographic evidence for fair human oversight.

Computer Vision Methodology

Uses OpenCV's optimized Haar Feature-based Cascade Classifiers. Incoming frames are resized and histogram-equalized. If face_count == 0 for consecutive cycles, a NO_FACE event triggers. If face_count > 1, a MULTIPLE_FACES high-severity violation is logged and snapshot evidence is captured.

Analyzes facial aspect ratios and horizontal asymmetry of eye coordinates relative to the face box boundary. Consecutive sustained off-center poses trigger LOOKING_AWAY warnings without penalizing natural micro-glances.

Extracts the ocular region, applies a Gaussian blur, and calculates the minimum intensity location representing the pupil. Relative horizontal displacement provides an approximate gaze vector (SCREEN / LEFT / RIGHT).

Scope & Evaluation

Key Advantages
  • Low computational footprint (runs on normal student laptops without GPU).
  • Minimal network bandwidth via periodic frame sampling.
  • Ethical proctoring policy: assisted flags rather than automatic disqualifications.
  • Comprehensive candidate audit timeline with screenshot evidence.
Limitations & Future Scope
  • Lighting variations and reflections from spectacles may affect gaze precision.
  • Future upgrade: Deep learning models (e.g., MediaPipe Face Mesh) for fine 3D iris tracking.
  • Future upgrade: Secondary mobile phone camera pairing for 360-degree room monitoring.
