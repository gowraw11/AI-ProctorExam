Core Capabilities

Intelligent Examination Architecture

Built with robust computer vision pipelines and a modern full-stack web stack to deliver an uncompromising examination environment.

OpenCV Face Detection

Continuously detects the candidate's face. Flags violations when no face is present or when multiple individuals enter the camera field of view.

Head Pose & Gaze Analysis

Tracks sustained head rotation and diverted eye gaze away from the examination screen with temporal smoothing to prevent false positives.

Dynamic Risk Engine

Computes cumulative risk scores dynamically based on event severity weights, categorizing sessions into Low, Medium, High, and Critical risk tiers.

Tab & Fullscreen Guard

Enforces full-screen mode and detects browser tab switching, clipboard operations (copy/cut/paste), and right-click context attempts.

Auto-Save & Timed Submit

Candidate responses are asynchronously persisted in real-time. Server-side countdown timers guarantee unforgeable automated submissions upon expiry.

Evidence & Audit Reports

Stores photographic webcam evidence for serious anomalies, provides interactive audit timelines, and produces CSV and PDF reports.

Security & Ethics

AI-Assisted Human Review, Not Blind Disqualification

In strict compliance with educational testing ethics, our proctoring engine flags potential suspicious activity rather than making irreversible accusations. Every flagged incident includes confidence metrics and optional photographic snapshots for human administrative review.

  • Zero Continuous Video Uploads: Only periodic lightweight frame samples (1-2 FPS) are transmitted to ensure minimal bandwidth and student privacy.
  • Data Privacy Notice: Photographs are solely captured when severe security threshold events trigger, stored locally in protected storage.
  • Configurable False-Positive Tolerances: Admins can tune sensitivity weights and customize pass/fail policies.
